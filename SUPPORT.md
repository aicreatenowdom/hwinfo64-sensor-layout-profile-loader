# Support for HW Info 64 Profile Loader

Contact **AI Creations Now Software Development** at [info@aicreatenow.com](mailto:info@aicreatenow.com) or **1-866-315-4750**.

[Official product page and instructions](https://aicreatenow.com/hwinfo64loader.html)

## Help us reproduce the problem

Include the loader version, HWiNFO64 version, Windows version and whether HWiNFO64 is installed or portable. Describe the profile operation and exact error. A screenshot of the affected window is useful.

Keep passwords, access tokens, license keys and private customer information out of public issues and screenshots. Send purchase or delivery questions directly by email.

## Before contacting support

- Confirm whether the selected HWiNFO64 executable is the installed or portable copy you intend to use.
- State whether saving or loading failed and whether both actions used the same Windows account.
